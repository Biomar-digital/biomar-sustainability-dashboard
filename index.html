How this was built

  • Source: GA4 Free form export, filtered to the Our Promise URL slug in every language.
  • Period: April–October 2026. January–March is left out because of GA4 tracking issues. October was added from a separate export and mapped with the same rules; the Philippines site (/en-ph), new in October, is included as its own market.
  • Global = www.biomar.com (and biomar.com) with no country folder; /en/our-promise is treated as the global Our Promise page.
  • Translated subpages are mapped to the English page they correspond to (e.g. klimaindsats → Climate Action, vores-loefte → Our Promise).
  • Circular & Restorative includes its subpages and URL variants, per the wildcard in the brief.
  • Excluded on request: biomar.ru and any Umbraco domain. Also excluded: localhost, Google Translate, encoded tracking URLs, malformed URLs, and biomar.cn rows showing another market's URL.
  • Active users are not shown as totals: GA4 counts the same person once per month and page, so adding them up overcounts.
  • Engagement time per view = User engagement (seconds) ÷ Views.
  • Average session duration is GA4's whole-visit length for sessions that included the page (not time on the page). Combined figures are weighted by sessions.
  • Sustainability pages: Our Promise, Our Blue Journey, Climate Action, Circular & Restorative, BioSustain, Impact parameters, Sustainability masterclass and Blue Impact. Benchmark pages: Enable People, Our Story, Better Feed, Power, Above & Beyond, Responsible sourcing and Our Operations. Each page counts its own URL only, never pages beneath it. All in every language (slugs from the sites' hreflang tags, plus alimento-innovador for Better Feed in Chile, Ecuador and Costa Rica, and the Feed Services folder names for Blue Impact and Power, and translated Impact parameters URLs such as impact-parametre). Other subpages count only in the whole-section chart.
